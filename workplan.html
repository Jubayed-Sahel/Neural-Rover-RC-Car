ESP32-CAM + Video Streaming

Get live video from the rover to your laptop

  • Flash CameraWebServer firmware on ESP32-CAM
  • Connect to WiFi and get local IP address
  • Access live MJPEG stream from laptop browser
  • Verify stream stability and resolution (640x480)
✅ Deliverable: Live video stream visible on laptop over WiFi

Week 5 · Aug 6

Pan-Tilt + Raised Camera Mount

Get the camera to face-level height

  • Build ~80cm rigid stalk (PVC pipe or carbon fiber)
  • Mount pan-tilt servos at the top
  • Attach ESP32-CAM to pan-tilt head
  • Control tilt via PWM from ESP32 to aim at face level
✅ Deliverable: Camera at ~80cm height, pan-tilt adjustable via PWM
📐 Rover sketch: chassis → stalk → pan-tilt → ESP32-CAM
Week 6 · Aug 13

MQTT + Alert System

Laptop detects → device reacts

  • Set up MQTT broker (HiveMQ Cloud free tier)
  • Python publisher: sends alert on detection
  • ESP32 subscriber: listens for incoming alerts
  • On alert: buzzer 3s + red LED ON + OLED shows "⚠ ALERT"
  • On clear: green LED + OLED shows "MONITORING..."
✅ Deliverable: Python → MQTT → ESP32 → buzzer/LED/OLED working end-to-end
