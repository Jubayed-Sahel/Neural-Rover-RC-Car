NEURAL ROVER - LAPTOP FACE RECOGNITION
=======================================

Use these files in this order:

1. Connect the laptop and ESP32-CAM to the same CAR hotspot.
2. Double-click: 01_INSTALL_GPU.bat
3. Double-click: 02_CHECK_SETUP.bat
4. Double-click: 03_TEST_CAMERA.bat
5. Double-click: 04_CAPTURE_FACES.bat
   - Enter a person's name.
   - Face the camera.
   - Press SPACE eight times while changing the face angle slightly.
   - Press Q when finished.
6. Repeat step 5 for every person.
7. Double-click: 05_RUN_RECOGNITION.bat

Stable ESP32-CAM stream:
http://rovercam.local:81/stream

If rovercam.local is not resolved by Windows, replace it in config.json
with the Camera stream (IP) printed by the ESP32 serial monitor.

Important:
- Close the camera stream in other browsers before recognition.
- The first model run needs internet to download buffalo_l.
- Press Q to close any camera window.
- Registered photographs and recognition logs stay on this laptop.

For more information, open README.md.
