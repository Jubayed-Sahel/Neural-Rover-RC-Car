Find the demo device IP addresses
  • Tapo camera appears in Tapo Device Info on Phone 2. Enter its current IP under Devices & network.
  • Demo laptop appears under Connect phone. You can also run ipconfig and read the Wi-Fi IPv4 address.
  • Main ESP32 is listed by MAC on Phone 1. Use the laptop to match that MAC to its current IP. See DEMO_RUNBOOK.md for the PowerShell command.

Recheck addresses after restarting CAR. The full rehearsal is in DEMO_RUNBOOK.md in the project folder.

  1. Enable your phone’s 2.4 GHz hotspot. The current firmware expects CAR, password car123456.
  2. Connect the laptop, Tapo and ESP32 to that hotspot. Configure the camera’s Wi-Fi in the Tapo app beforehand.
  3. Power the Tapo with its correct adapter. Bring laptop and rover power supplies.
  4. Find the camera and ESP32 addresses on the hotspot. Update Devices & network if they changed.
