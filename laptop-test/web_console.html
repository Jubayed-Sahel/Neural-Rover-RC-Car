Your portable setup

  1. Enable your phone’s 2.4 GHz hotspot. The current firmware expects CAR, password car123456.
  2. Connect the laptop, Tapo and ESP32 to that hotspot. Configure the camera’s Wi-Fi in the Tapo app beforehand.
  3. Power the Tapo with its correct adapter. Bring laptop and rover power supplies.
  4. Find the camera and ESP32 addresses on the hotspot. Update Devices & network if they changed.
  5. Open this workspace, start the camera, connect the rover, and pair the phone.
  6. With wheels raised, check release-to-stop, STOP, control disconnection and driving while video is stopped.
