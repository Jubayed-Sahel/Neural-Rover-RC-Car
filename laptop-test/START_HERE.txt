13_START_PHONE_REMOTE.bat  Open the dashboard with paired local phone control.

In People, enter name/ID/role, Choose & check photos, review, then Save.
Re-enroll any old SFace profiles. Use Add photos to improve a current profile.
For live enrollment, stop the dashboard camera and restart the app afterwards.
Start with Preview only to test the camera, then turn it off for recognition.
In Rover controls, enter the MAIN ESP32 IP, Connect, and hold to drive.
Release, STOP or Escape stops. Test with the wheels raised first.
All devices must share a reachable network. Current controller expects CAR.
Video failure does not disable phone driving. The control link must still work.

UNIVERSITY DEMO: use the exact phone hotspot tested at home, on 2.4 GHz.
Join Tapo, laptop and ESP32 to it before demo day; power alone is not enough.
After restarting the hotspot, check camera/ESP32 IPs and the laptop page address.
Bring the original camera adapter, chargers, power strip and rover batteries.
See TAPO_START_HERE.md for hotspot setup and the cold-start rehearsal.
